Privacy Policy

MDRS School — web & Android (Trusted Web Activity)

This page describes how the MDRS School app (the website you use in the browser, and the Android app that opens the same website) handles information. The school may update this text from time to time; the “Last updated” date is shown at the bottom.

What the app is

The Android app is a Trusted Web Activity: it displays the official MDRS School website. No separate “native” copy of your school data is stored inside the Android wrapper beyond normal browser-style caching controlled by the site (including optional offline support).

Information you may provide

Depending on what your school has turned on inside the site, you might submit things like feedback, votes, or sign-in details. Those actions happen on the same website as in the browser and are processed according to the school’s own systems (for example secure cloud services the school has configured).

Data storage & security

  • Traffic between your device and the site uses HTTPS (encrypted in transit).
  • Optional offline or “app-like” features may store parts of the site on your device so pages can load faster or when the network is poor — similar to other progressive web apps.

Children & students

The app is intended for the school community. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about student data, please contact the school directly.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact MDRS School administration using the contact details your school provides (website, office, or email). You can return to the home page from here.
